RisingAMP
Phase 14 vision

Ask

One box that answers questions about a job in plain language. It never adds up a number itself. It decides which question to ask your own code, and your code does the maths.

The problem

You can see the totals. You cannot ask about them.

RisingAMP will tell you a job is at 11.9% margin and that three invoices have no date. It cannot tell you what you have paid Metro Consulting this year, whether carpentry is running over the estimate, or what the contract says about retention. Those answers exist in your data. There is just no way to ask for them.

"What have we paid Metro?"

Open History. Filter. Scroll. Add it up in your head, or in a calculator, and hope.

"Are we over on carpentry?"

Cost Plan on one screen, History on another, and the comparison happens in your head.

"What did the contract say?"

Files, open the PDF, scroll, read. Assuming you remember which one it was in.

Where it lives

In the box you already have

Not a chat bubble in the corner. Phase 13 already made the command palette answer spend questions with no AI at all. Phase 14 puts a model in front of the same queries so you can ask in your own words instead of guessing the keyword.

How it works

The model picks the question. Your code finds the answer.

This is the whole design, and it is the opposite of how most of these are built. The AI never reads your ledger and never adds anything up.

The messy library

Embed everything, retrieve chunks, let the model summarise. This is what most AI features do.

It throws away the structure you spent five phases building, then tries to rebuild it by guesswork. The model adds up numbers from fragments. It gets one wrong. Nobody notices. Ask again tomorrow and the answer is different.

The tidy workplace

The model chooses one of about ten precise queries and fills the blanks. Phase 13 built those queries.

The number comes from the same code that draws your Overview, so the assistant and the dashboard cannot disagree. Same question, same answer, every time. And every answer can show its working.

What it can answer

Ten questions, asked any way you like

The queries already exist and are already read-only. Phase 14 only adds the routing, so the surface it can touch is fixed and small by design.

No invented figures, ever. The same rule the Overview already follows: if the data to compute something honestly does not exist, say so and show what does. A dash beats a fake zero.
No arithmetic on document text. It can quote what a contract says. It will never add up numbers it read out of a PDF, because that is exactly where a confident wrong answer comes from.
It shows the shape of what it can do. When a question is outside the ten, it offers the closest one it can actually answer rather than improvising.
It never writes. No creating an expense, no voiding an invoice, no editing anything. Answering is the whole job.

On a phone

Answers are the app's own screens, not a wall of text

A number in prose cannot be tapped. Ask about unpaid invoices and you get real invoice rows that open. Ask about a trade and you get the trade board. Ask for a document and it opens in the viewer Phase 9 built.
It knows where you are. Inside a job it answers about that job, with a chip you can clear to go company-wide. Most of the ambiguity is gone before the model sees the question.
Built for one hand and one bar. Same tap targets and safe areas as Phase 7. It streams, so you see it thinking rather than staring at a spinner.
Two to four seconds, honestly. A model call plus a round trip to a database that is not in this country. It says "checking the ledger" rather than pretending to be instant.

Deliberately not building

Named so it does not arrive by accident

Writing anything

No creating, editing, voiding or deleting. A different risk class, and it needs a confirmation flow nobody has designed.

Doing its own maths

Every figure comes from the query layer. If a number needs a new calculation, that is a new query, reviewed and tested.

Embedding your ledger

No vector store over expenses or invoices. Retrieval is only for words inside documents.

Advice

It reports what happened. It does not tell you whether to take a job or what to charge.

Reading images

Receipt OCR stays narrow and separate. No general image reading.

Answering everything

Ten questions, done properly. Unbounded scope is how this becomes a project that never ships.

The one rule that cannot bend. This is a tool people put their money records into. One visibly wrong figure and they stop believing all of them. So the assistant may phrase a number, link to it and explain it, but it may never be the thing that calculated it.
