Phase 9 vision

Job Files

Every document for a job in one place, findable in ten seconds, and a handover pack at the end. A filing cabinet with labelled drawers, not a hard drive.

The problem

The app answers every question about money and none about paper

RisingAMP can tell you a job is making 11.9% and that three invoices are missing dates. It cannot tell you where the engineer's certificate for the slab is, or whether the variation for that extra window was ever signed.

"Where's the slab cert?"

Somewhere in an email thread from March, or on someone's phone.

"Did they sign the variation?"

The invoice went out. Whether the paperwork backs it up is a guess.

"Client wants the handover pack."

A day of hunting through inboxes at the exact moment you are busiest.

The screen

Search first, filter second, folders never

Files belong to a job and carry a type. There is no folder tree, so nothing can be filed in the wrong place.

Compressed on the way in. The receipt uploader already shrinks a photo to 1920px at 0.8 quality. A 9 MB phone photo becomes about 400 KB before it ever leaves the device.
A thumbnail is generated and stored beside it. Every list and grid renders the 320px thumbnail, never the original. This is the single decision that keeps the bill small.
The document's own date, not the upload date. A certificate issued in March and uploaded in August is a March document. Sorting by upload date buries it.
25 MB a file, and no video. One two-minute site clip costs more to store and serve than a thousand compressed photos.

The integration

Files feed the panel that already exists

"What needs you today" reads honest signals from stored data and says nothing when it cannot. Documents give it four more, in exactly that spirit.

2 files still typed "Other"

The existing rule holds: if the data to compute something honestly does not exist, the panel says nothing. A check that fires on every job on day one gets ignored, and takes the real warnings down with it.

Where it would get expensive

A gallery rendering 100 full-size images costs 40 MB a page view. The same gallery on 40 KB thumbnails costs 4 MB. Egress is the bill, not storage.

So: three rules

Compress every image on upload. Serve a thumbnail in every list. No video in this phase. Hold those and the whole thing stays under a couple of dollars a month.

The one decision to hold

No folders. Not now, not later.

The instinct is a file explorer with a folder tree. It is the wrong shape, and this is the place to overrule the instinct.

Why folders lose

Two people file the same certificate in two places. Someone makes "Docs" next to "Documents". Six months on, nobody can find it. That is the same disease as 72 Centenary Drive / Road / Rd, wearing a different costume, and Phase 5 spent a whole phase curing it.

What replaces them

A job, a type from a fixed list, a name, the document's date, and an optional link to the invoice or expense it belongs to. Finding a file is search plus a type filter, and neither can be filed wrong.
