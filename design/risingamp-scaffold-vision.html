4 The docket, on the truck tailgate, in sixty seconds

Count it on, count it off. The variance writes itself.

The driver counts the return against what went out. Anything short turns red on its own, priced at replacement, with two honest choices: charge the builder or write it off. Either way the ledger stays true, and the yard count tonight matches reality.

This is the whole trick. The Gear Ledger is only as good as its ins and outs, and the docket is how scaffolders already think. We just made it signable on glass, with the maths done.
